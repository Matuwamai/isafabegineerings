# Images: Isafab Engineering

## Real photos (preferred)

Real photos of his work live in `public/images/work/` and are listed in `src/data/work.js` with their captions.
They appear in the Gallery, the Home page "Recent work" section and on the matching service pages.

To add a new photo:
1. Save it in `public/images/work/` with a short descriptive name, e.g. `sliding-gate-nakuru.jpg`.
2. Add one line for it in `src/data/work.js`: file name, size in pixels, category, caption, and the service it
   belongs to (optional).

Take photos in daylight, with the finished work filling the frame and the site tidied up. Before-and-after pairs
work very well.
